# OrbitMobile Billing and Refunds Guide

## Bill cycle

Postpaid and prepaid-with-auto-recharge accounts align to a **28-day bill cycle** anchored on the activation date. Invoices appear in self-care within 24 hours of cycle close.

## Payment methods

Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, ACH bank transfer (postpaid only), and OrbitMobile account credit.

## Late payment

Postpaid accounts with balance unpaid **10 days** after due date receive a friendly reminder. Service suspension may occur after **20 days**; restoration fee is **$5** per line.

## Refund eligibility

| Scenario | Refund |
|----------|--------|
| Duplicate recharge within 48 hours | Full duplicate amount |
| Plan downgrade mid-cycle | Pro-rated credit on next invoice |
| Device return within 14 days (unopened) | Full device charge |
| Roaming package unused | No refund once activated |

## How to request a refund

1. Open a billing case in self-care under **Payments & refunds**.
2. Provide transaction ID and reason.
3. Refunds to card take **5–10 business days**; account credit is instant when approved.

## Disputes

Billing disputes must be filed within **60 days** of the invoice date. OrbitMobile responds within 15 business days.

## Tax

Sales tax is calculated based on the service address on file. Tax-exempt organizations must upload certificate **before** the next bill cycle.
