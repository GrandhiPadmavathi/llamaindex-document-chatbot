# OrbitMobile Prepaid Plans FAQ

**Document ID:** OM-PRE-2025-01  
**Last updated:** 2025-06-01

## Plan overview

| Plan | Monthly price | Data | Voice |
|------|---------------|------|-------|
| Orbit Lite | $15 | 2 GB | 500 minutes |
| Orbit Plus | $25 | 8 GB | unlimited domestic |
| Orbit Max | $40 | 25 GB | unlimited domestic + 100 international minutes |

All prepaid plans renew every 30 days from the last successful recharge.

## Recharge methods

Customers may recharge using:

1. OrbitMobile self-care website or mobile app (credit/debit card).
2. Authorized retail vouchers (12-digit code).
3. Auto-recharge when balance falls below $5 (optional, requires saved payment method).

## Data rollover

Unused data from **Orbit Plus** and **Orbit Max** rolls over for one billing cycle only, up to 5 GB. **Orbit Lite** does not support rollover.

## International roaming

International roaming is **off by default** on prepaid lines. Enable it in self-care before travel. Orbit Max includes a roaming starter pack: 500 MB and 30 minutes in Zone A countries for 7 days after first use abroad.

## Support

Prepaid support: chat and IVR 24/7. Phone agent hours: 08:00–22:00 local time.
