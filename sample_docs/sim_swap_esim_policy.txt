OrbitMobile — SIM Swap and eSIM Policy
Policy version: 3.2 (2025-04-15)

PHYSICAL SIM SWAP
- SIM swaps require identity verification: account PIN plus one-time passcode to the registered email OR in-store government ID check.
- Online SIM swap requests are processed within 4 business hours after verification.
- After swap, the old SIM stops working within 15 minutes.
- Maximum 2 SIM swaps per line per rolling 30-day period unless fraud team approves an exception.

eSIM
- eSIM is supported on OrbitMobile-certified devices listed on the device compatibility page.
- One active eSIM profile per line; physical SIM and eSIM cannot be active simultaneously on the same line.
- eSIM QR codes expire 24 hours after issuance. Request a new QR from self-care if expired.
- Transferring eSIM to a new phone requires removing the profile on the old device first, then scanning a new QR.

FRAUD HOLD
- High-risk swap attempts trigger a 24-hour security hold and SMS alert to the account owner.
- Customers on fraud hold must visit a retail store with photo ID to complete the swap.

NOT SUPPORTED
- SIM swap via social media direct messages.
- Sharing eSIM QR codes with third parties (QR is single-use).
